One image, many headlines

Lumeo ran the same car-selfie photo with 4 headlines across 3 pages for two months. The picture wins; headlines are the cheap test.

Mascots and memes last longest

Primal Storm’s cartoon jar ads have been live 157 days. Absurd, brand-safe, never fatigued. Same tone that wins for us.

Comparison sells volume

Drops vs. overflowing glass, dribble vs. rope. It’s the same idea as our #1 ad, Empty Glass vs Full Splash.

What got them banned

Fake press mastheads, nudity, and explicit fluid shots. About a quarter of Lumeo’s ads were removed. The prompts swap only those parts out.

How the rank works. Score = days the ad ran + 8 per extra copy of the creative + 6 per extra headline/size variant + 20 if it is still live today. Meta doesn’t publish sales, so runtime and duplication are the best public signals that an ad makes money. Most Lumeo ads stopped on 14–16 Sep because Meta took their pages down, not because the ads stopped working; they relaunched on a new page on 23 Sep.
